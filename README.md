# Privacy Policy for Grocify

**Last Updated: 25 June 2026**

## Introduction

Grocify ("we", "our", or "the app") is a grocery shopping, price comparison, and loyalty card management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Grocify does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that identifies you personally.

## Information We Collect

### Camera Access
- **What:** We access your device camera to scan barcodes and QR codes on loyalty cards and products.
- **Why:** To quickly add loyalty cards to your digital wallet and to look up product pricing information.
- **Storage:** Scanned barcode data is stored locally on your device only.

### Location Data
- **What:** We access your device location when you search for nearby stores and prices.
- **Why:** To show you relevant store locations, distances, and localised pricing where available.
- **Storage:** Your **exact location is used in real time only and is never stored or retained.** To understand general usage patterns, an **approximate location (your city or province only — never your exact coordinates)** may be included in the anonymous analytics described below.

### Loyalty Card Information
- **What:** Card names, barcode numbers, and card images you manually enter or scan.
- **Why:** To display your loyalty cards for use at checkout.
- **Storage:** All loyalty card data is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or shared.

### Pricing and Product Data Retrieval
- **What:** When you use Grocify's price comparison features, the app retrieves publicly available pricing, product, and promotions information from retailers.
- **Why:** To display accurate, up-to-date prices and specials across multiple retailers so you can compare and make informed shopping decisions.
- **How:** These requests are made through Grocify's own secure infrastructure on your behalf and contain **no personally identifiable information** — only general location context (such as your area) needed to show prices and stores near you.
- **Storage:** Retrieved pricing data is used in real time to display results and is not linked to your identity.

### Analytics
We collect **anonymous** usage analytics through two services: **Google Firebase Analytics**, and **Grocify's own analytics infrastructure**. This helps us understand how the app is used and where to improve it.

This anonymous data includes:
- General usage activity — for example, which features are used, searches performed, and screens viewed;
- Session information — such as how often and how long the app is used;
- General device information — such as device model, operating system version, language, and country/region;
- Your **approximate location** — city or province only, never your exact coordinates.

To measure usage without identifying you, the app generates a **random, anonymous identifier** that is stored on your device. This identifier is **not** linked to your name, email, phone number, or any account, and you can reset it at any time by reinstalling the app.

**No advertising identifiers are collected.** All of this information is aggregated, is used solely to improve the app experience and understand usage patterns, and **cannot be used to identify you individually.**

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage Only:** Your personal data — loyalty cards, shopping lists, and preferences — is stored exclusively on your device.
- **No Personal Data in the Cloud:** We do **not** upload your personal data (loyalty cards, list contents, or your exact location) to any server.
- **Anonymous Analytics:** We **do** collect anonymous, aggregated usage analytics — via Firebase and on our own infrastructure — as described in the Analytics section above. This data cannot identify you.
- **No Account Required:** Grocify does not require user accounts or registration.
- **Encryption:** Sensitive data is encrypted using your device's secure storage.

## Third-Party Services

The app connects to external services to:
- Display product information and pricing;
- Show store locations;
- Retrieve current specials and promotions;
- Look up product details by barcode.

Pricing and product requests are made through Grocify's own infrastructure on your behalf and **do not include any personally identifiable information** — only general location context when searching for prices and stores near you.

The app uses **Firebase Analytics** (a Google service) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data. For more information on how Google processes this data, see Google's Privacy Policy.

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT**:
- Sell or share your **personal** information with third parties;
- Share your personal information with advertisers;
- Track your behaviour for marketing purposes.

We may produce and share **anonymous, aggregated market insights** — for example, general pricing or shopping trends across retailers — derived from usage data. Such insights are fully anonymised, contain **no personal information**, and **cannot identify any individual**.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Delete your data at any time by deleting individual loyalty cards or lists, or by uninstalling the app;
- Deny permissions for camera or location (some features will be limited);
- Reset your anonymous analytics identifier by reinstalling the app;
- Use the app offline for viewing saved loyalty cards.

To request information about the data we hold or to raise a privacy concern, please contact us using the details below.

## Children's Privacy

Grocify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
